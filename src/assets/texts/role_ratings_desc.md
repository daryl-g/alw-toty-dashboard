**Significance range explained**:

- `[0.8, 0.9]`: Non-negotiables for the position.
- `[0.7]`: Must-haves, but debatable and subjective depending on the player's preferred role and who you ask.
- `[0.5, 0.6]`: Good-to-haves, not super important for the position.
- `[0.3, 0.4]`: Ehh, they are there too, I guess? Add a bit of value but won't change the ratings much if they are removed.

**Note**:

- This is only using the player's performance stats to evaluate how good that player might be in the chosen position.
- **Position** and **Role** are different concepts. For example:
  - **Position**: Left-back; **Roles**: Defensive full-back, Attacking full-back, Inverted full-back, Playmaking full-back, etc.
