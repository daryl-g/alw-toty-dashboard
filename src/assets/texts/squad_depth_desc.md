- Squad Depth is determined based on each player's playing time and match appearances from the 2024-25 season.
- This page can also be used to see players' contract expiry and identify positions where teams will need to find replacements.

**Notes**:

- Player's contract expiry year data are up-to-date as of September 1st, 2025 using data from [Soccerdonna](https://www.soccerdonna.de/en/a-league-women/startseite/wettbewerb_WL.html), [A-League's official transfer tracker](https://aleagues.com.au/news/a-league-women-25-26-transfers-transfer-news-signings-ins-outs-every-club-details-rumours-latest-done-deals/), and news from clubs' website.
- Players who have left their club are assumed to have their contract expired after the 2024-25 season is finished unless stated otherwise.
- Player positions are not guaranteed to be 100% correct, the positions are gathered and assumed using Soccerdonna's classification and FBRef's match logs. This is a known problem with women's football data and something that I have written about [here](https://www.talking-tactics.com/i/164710406/the-data) if you are interested in reading about the problems that I faced while doing the pre-processing step for the dataset.
